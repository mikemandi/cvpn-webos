# CVPN webOS — Brand and icon usage policy

[English](BRAND_POLICY.md) · [Русский](BRAND_POLICY.ru.md) · [Proprietary license](LICENSE)

This policy concerns **CVPN, CVPN webOS, CVPN Connect, PRIVATE NETWORK**, original
CVPN logos, application icons, interface identifiers and distinct visual assets,
but only to the extent the applicable project rightsholders actually own or
control them. It does not assert registered trademark status or ownership of
third-party graphics, fonts, product names or other marks.

## Uses that are welcome without advance permission

- Truthful statements referring to CVPN webOS, linking to its official GitHub
  repository, or accurately describing compatibility and supported VPN clients.
- Independent user reviews, tutorials, press coverage, research, comparisons
  and bug reports that clearly do not suggest official approval or affiliation.
- Displaying unmodified official screenshots and icons for accurately
  identifying the application in the webOS Homebrew Channel catalog and
  other genuine app directory listings, review articles and download pages
  that link to the **official** releases.
- Showing the app's existing icons and interface during ordinary end-user use.

Keep attribution where appropriate and do not remove source notices.

## Uses that require written permission

- Naming or branding an independently distributed VPN app or service
  "CVPN", "CVPN webOS", "CVPN Connect", or a confusingly similar name.
- Replacing the app's original publisher and redistributing a clone or a
  modified build using the CVPN logo/visual identity.
- Using original CVPN logos, app icons, badges or design assets as the
  branding of another software product, business or paid service.
- Implying sponsorship, certification, endorsement or partnership by CVPN
  where none exists.
- Merchandise or advertising built primarily around CVPN visual assets.

This policy does not prevent legally protected nominative, descriptive,
comparative, journalistic, interoperability or fair uses, and does not
override the GitHub Terms of Service or third-party license rights.
